01 OUR STORY

Made for the
next step.

For many learners, English is more than another subject. It's a way to connect with new ideas, explore opportunities, and express what matters.

Lingolift is an English-learning mobile application created with Sri Lankan students and beginners in mind. We bring together a thoughtful learning path, grammar development, vocabulary building, and AI English learning assistance in one approachable place.

This website is here to introduce the brand and share useful ideas. The lessons themselves live inside the Android application.
