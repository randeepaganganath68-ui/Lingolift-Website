A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Getting to know
Lingolift.

Wondering if Lingolift is right for you? Here's a little more about the app.

Who is Lingolift for?

Lingolift is designed for Sri Lankan students and English beginners who want to build their grammar, vocabulary, and confidence step by step.

Where do I learn my lessons?

All lessons are inside the Lingolift Android application. This website introduces the app and shares helpful articles; it is not a lesson platform.

Do I need to be good at English to get started?

No. Lingolift is for learners beginning their English journey as well as those who want to strengthen their foundations.

How can I get the Android app?

Visit our download page for the latest availability information. The Google Play link will appear here once it is officially available.
